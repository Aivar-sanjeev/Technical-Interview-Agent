# Machine Learning Engineer — Ranking & Retrieval

We need an ML Engineer to improve search and recommendation quality using modern deep learning and classical ML.

## Responsibilities
- Train, evaluate, and deploy ranking and embedding models at scale.
- Build reproducible training pipelines, feature stores, and offline/online metrics.
- Work with product and backend teams to integrate models behind low-latency APIs.
- Monitor drift, fairness constraints, and model performance in production.

## Requirements
- MS or PhD in CS/ML or equivalent experience; 3+ years shipping ML to production.
- Strong Python; PyTorch or TensorFlow; familiarity with transformers.
- Experience with vector search, ANN indexes, or learning-to-rank systems.
- Solid statistics and experimental design (A/B tests, confidence intervals).
- Clear written communication of trade-offs and risks.

## Nice to have
- GPU training at scale; ONNX/TorchScript; model compression.
- Experience with Spark or BigQuery for large-scale feature engineering.
